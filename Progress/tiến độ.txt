TIEN DO DU AN POS DESKTOP
Cap nhat gan nhat: 2026-05-16

1. Trang thai hien tai
- Du an la ung dung POS desktop viet bang Python + PyQt6.
- App dung mot cua so chinh PosMainWindow va dieu huong noi dung bang QStackedWidget.
- Sidebar ben trai hien cac khu vuc theo quyen: dashboard, POS Terminal, Product Management va cac trang admin.
- Database runtime la SQLite, file chinh: pos.db.
- App hien co cac nhom chuc nang:
  + Dang nhap, giu phien dang nhap, dang xuat.
  + POS Terminal de ban hang, thanh toan, in/xem hoa don.
  + Product Management de quan ly danh muc san pham.
  + Admin: users, registers, reports, audit logs, settings.
- Cac trang co the tu reload du lieu thong qua signal chung app_data_changed.

2. Cac thay doi da sua / da bo sung
- Chuc nang cu: App tung dung viec xoa barcode sau khi ban de gia lap het hang.
  Chuc nang moi: Barcode la ma nhan dien co dinh, stock_qty la nguon ton kho chinh.
  Da sua: Bo logic xoa barcode sau sale, moi lan ban chi tru stock_qty.

- Chuc nang cu: Barcode da ban van co the quet lai va di tiep.
  Chuc nang moi: Barcode het hang/da ban bi chan ngay.
  Da sua: Them kiem tra khi quet, kiem tra lai truoc khi thanh toan, va them lop validate o database de khong ban lot khi ton kho thay doi.

- Chuc nang cu: Gio hang tung chan quet lap lai cung mot barcode.
  Chuc nang moi: Quet lai cung barcode se tang so luong cua san pham.
  Da sua: Gio hang quay ve xu ly theo product_id, phu hop voi barcode co dinh va ton kho theo so luong.

- Chuc nang cu: Shift + F5 chi refresh, khong co phan hoi giao dien.
  Chuc nang moi: Reset co thong bao thanh cong.
  Da sua: Them toast/animation "Reset successful" va status bar message.

- Chuc nang cu: Dong app la phai dang nhap lai.
  Chuc nang moi: Giu phien dang nhap sau khi tat app.
  Da sua: Them session persistence; neu bat "Keep me logged in" thi mo app se vao lai tai khoan truoc do.

- Chuc nang cu: Khong co nut dang xuat.
  Chuc nang moi: Co nut Logout tren sidebar.
  Da sua: Them nut Logout kem icon, xoa session va quay lai man hinh login.

- Chuc nang cu: Hoa don co the hien truoc khi thanh toan hoan tat.
  Chuc nang moi: Hoa don chi duoc tao/hien sau khi thanh toan thanh cong.
  Da sua: Sua lai luong payment -> dialog accepted -> moi finalize payment va hien receipt.

- Chuc nang cu: Product Management chia doi man hinh, form ben trai va bang ben phai.
  Chuc nang moi: Product Management chi con man danh sach, thao tac them/sua bang popup modal.
  Da sua: Bo panel Product Details ben trai, tao ProductDialog dung chung cho Add Product va Edit Product.

- Chuc nang cu: Sua/xoa san pham phu thuoc vao form chung va dong dang chon.
  Chuc nang moi: Moi dong trong bang co nut Edit va Delete rieng.
  Da sua: Them action buttons theo tung dong, modal Edit tu dien du lieu cu, sau Save/Delete bang tu reload.

3. Cau truc file chinh
- main.py
  Entry point chinh; tao QApplication, khoi tao auth DB, khoi phuc session neu co, mo PosMainWindow.

- pos_terminal.py
  Wrapper de lenh chay cu van hoat dong.

- login/login_window.py
  Dang nhap, verify user, session persistence, settings auth, cash shift helpers.

- database/db.py
  Helper database chung: products, barcodes, sales, ton kho theo stock_qty, validate sale, migration inventory model.

- pos_terminal/pos_window.py
  Main window, sidebar, POS Terminal, cart, payment, receipt preview, reset toast, logout.

- product_management/product_management_window.py
  Product list page va ProductDialog modal cho Add/Edit Product.

- admin/*
  Dashboard, users, registers, reports, audit logs, settings.

- ui/icon_manager.py
  Quan ly icon dung chung cho toan app.

- Progress/tiến độ.txt
  Tai lieu tien do tieng Viet.

- Progress/POS DESKTOP PROJECT PROGRESS.txt
  Tai lieu tien do tieng Anh.

4. Dong bo du lieu toan app
- PosMainWindow co signal chung:
  app_data_changed = pyqtSignal()
- Cac trang ghi DB co the emit data_changed sau khi save/update/delete.
- Luong refresh:
  page.data_changed -> notify_app_data_changed() -> app_data_changed.emit()
- MainWindow tu tim ham reload cua tung trang:
  + reload_data
  + load_dashboard_data
  + load_products
  + load_users
  + load_registers
  + load_settings
  + load_report
  + load_logs
- Shift + F5:
  + Refresh du lieu toan app.
  + Hien toast reset thanh cong.

5. Database hien tai
- Cac bang chinh:
  + products
  + product_barcodes
  + sales
  + sale_items
  + sale_payments
  + sync_queue
- products co cac truong nhu barcode, sku, stock_qty, active, updated_at, image_path.
- product_barcodes luu nhieu barcode cho mot san pham, co is_primary.
- sales va sale_items luu lich su giao dich.
- Co them logic:
  + get_available_stock()
  + is_barcode_available()
  + was_barcode_sold()
  + ensure_sale_items_available()
- stock_qty la nguon su that cua ton kho.
- Da them migration mot lan de chuyen du lieu cu sang inventory model moi.
- Ly do khong xoa barcode sau moi lan ban:
  + Trong ban le thong thuong, barcode dai dien cho loai san pham, khong dai dien cho tung don vi rieng le.
  + Vi du nhieu chai Coca cung loai co the dung chung mot barcode.
  + Neu ban 1 chai ma xoa barcode thi chai tiep theo cung loai se khong quet duoc du van con hang.
  + So luong con lai phai duoc quan ly bang stock_qty.
  + Neu can theo doi tung mon rieng nhu dien thoai, ve, gift card, IMEI/serial thi nen tao bang rieng kieu serial_items/inventory_units, khong dung barcode san pham de thay the.

6. POS Terminal hien tai
- Layout:
  + Sidebar trai.
  + Product grid o giua.
  + Cart + totals + action buttons ben phai.
- Product grid:
  + Search/scan barcode.
  + Card hien anh, ten, barcode, gia.
  + Enter de tim theo barcode/name.
- Cart:
  + Columns: Barcode, Name, Qty, Price, Total, Remove.
  + Qty co the sua neu hop le.
  + Quet lai cung barcode se tang qty cua san pham.
  + Gio hang kiem tra ton kho truoc khi thanh toan.
- Actions:
  + Pay
  + Split Payment
  + Cancel
- Sidebar:
  + Co nut Logout.

7. Thanh toan va hoa don
- Pay:
  + Mo Payment dialog.
  + Kiem tra ton kho truoc khi luu sale.
  + Chi khi dialog duoc Accept thi moi finalize payment.
- Split Payment:
  + Ho tro Cash + Card/Transfer.
  + Cung chi hien hoa don sau khi thanh toan thanh cong.
- Receipt Preview:
  + Hien sau sale thanh cong.
  + Gom payment method, item, total, tendered, change.
- Khong con luong preview hoa don truoc khi giao dich thanh cong.

8. Product Management hien tai
- Man chinh chi gom:
  + Search
  + Add Product
  + Bang san pham
  + Edit/Delete theo tung dong
- Add Product:
  + Mo ProductDialog modal.
  + Save xong dong popup va reload bang.
- Edit Product:
  + Mo ProductDialog modal.
  + Tu dien du lieu cu.
  + Save xong dong popup va reload bang.
- Modal van ho tro:

  + Nhieu barcode.

  + Stock Quantity.
  + Validate barcode trung.

9. Dang nhap va phien lam viec
- Login co toggle "Keep me logged in".
- Neu bat toggle:
  + user id duoc luu trong settings.
  + Mo app lan sau se tu khoi phuc user.
- Logout:
  + Xoa session.
  + Quay lai man login.

10. Cach chay app
- Cai moi truong:

13. Ghi chu cho nguoi tiep tuc code
- Han che viet SQL truc tiep trong UI; nen dat helper vao database/db.py.
- Page nao ghi DB thi nen emit data_changed sau khi thay doi.
- Page nao can refresh thi nen co reload_data().
- Neu sua POS thi xem pos_terminal/pos_window.py.
- Neu sua Product Management thi xem product_management/product_management_window.py.
- Neu sua login/session thi xem login/login_window.py.
- Neu sua schema/helper thi xem database/db.py.
- pos.db la runtime database, du lieu co the khac giua cac may.
